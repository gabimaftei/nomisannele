About us

A mother, a daughter and a pile of jeans

It started at home, in 2025, with a pile of jeans we could not bring ourselves to get rid of. Too good for the bin, too worn to keep wearing.

The first bag took twelve pairs and far longer than we expected. It is called Patchwork and it is still here, further down. Then came the second one, then the rest.

We work together, by hand. We cut, we sew, and when we do not like how it turned out we unpick it and start again. That is why we do not make many, and why every bag carries the seams and pockets of the jeans it came from.

“Upcycling is fashion that actually cares.”

How we make them

From worn jeans to finished bag

  1. 01

    We gather jeans

    From friends, from donations, from the back of wardrobes. We wash them and sort them by colour and texture.

  2. 02

    We cut

    We draw the pattern and cut piece by piece. Pockets, hems and labels stay where they were. They are what makes the difference.

  3. 03

    We sew

    With lining, inner pockets and a zip or a button. A bag takes anywhere from one pair of jeans to twelve.

  4. 04

    We send it to you

    You message us on Instagram, we sort out the details, we post it. You talk to us directly, with nobody in between.
